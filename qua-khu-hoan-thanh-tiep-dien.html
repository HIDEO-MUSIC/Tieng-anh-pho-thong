1. Cấu trúc

Khẳng định

S + had + been + V-ing

Phủ định

S + had + not + been + V-ing

Nghi vấn

Had + S + been + V-ing?

2. Cách dùng

1. Diễn tả một hành động đã bắt đầu và kéo dài liên tục trước một thời điểm hoặc một hành động khác trong quá khứ.

2. Nhấn mạnh khoảng thời gian hoặc tính liên tục của một hành động trước một thời điểm trong quá khứ.

3. Diễn tả nguyên nhân của một kết quả xảy ra trong quá khứ.

3. Dấu hiệu nhận biết

for – trong khoảng

since – kể từ

before – trước khi

until then – cho đến lúc đó

by the time – vào lúc

when – khi

all day – cả ngày

all morning – cả buổi sáng

4. Ví dụ

I had been studying English for two hours before dinner.

She had been working there for five years before she moved.

They had been playing football for an hour when it started to rain.

He was tired because he had been working all day.

5. Bài tập

Bài 1. Chia động từ trong ngoặc:

1. I __________ (study) English for two hours before dinner.

2. She __________ (work) there for five years before she moved.

3. They __________ (play) football for an hour when it started to rain.

4. We __________ (wait) for the bus for thirty minutes before it arrived.

5. He __________ (work) all day, so he was very tired.

Bài 2. Chọn đáp án đúng:

1. I ________ English for two hours before dinner.

A. studied

B. had been studying

C. have studied

2. She ________ there for five years before she moved.

A. had been working

B. worked

C. has worked

3. They ________ football for an hour when it started to rain.

A. played

B. were playing

C. had been playing

4. He was tired because he ________ all day.

A. had been working

B. worked

C. was working
